Pauline Pic holds the French agrégation in geography (2015) and taught history and geography at the lycée Stendhal in Grenoble in 2015–2016, before completing a PhD in geographical sciences at Université Laval (2016–2022) under the supervision of Frédéric Lasserre and Stéphane Roussel. Her thesis examines the scales of Arctic security. She then held a postdoctoral fellowship at the École supérieure d’études internationales of Université Laval (2022–2024), within the EPI chair led by Jean-Frédéric Morin, where she worked on governance processes in the Commons. Since 2026, she has been a professor at UQAR, where she holds a research chair in the geopolitics of seas and oceans.
