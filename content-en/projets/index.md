## Geopolitics of maritime data

An analysis of the conditions under which maritime data (satellite, oceanographic, and data from navigation or fishing) are produced, accessed and circulated, and of the power relations they reveal among states, scientific agencies, research institutions and private actors. This project is part of axis 3 of my research chair’s programme.

[See related publications](../publication/#all&area=geopolitique-donnees-maritimes)

## Arctic geopolitics and governance

A study of Arctic geopolitical dynamics and governance regimes: security, the law of the sea, the continental shelf, navigation, and the role of Indigenous and non-state actors. The project includes the special issue on Arctic geopolitics of <em>Études Inuit Studies</em>, co-edited with Karen Everett and Lisa Qiluqqi Koperqualuk.

[See related publications](../publication/#all&area=geopolitique-arctique)

## Governance of the Commons

An analysis of the governance of global common spaces, including outer space, drawing on empirical approaches to institutional arrangements.
