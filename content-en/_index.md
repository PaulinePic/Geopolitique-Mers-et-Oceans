Pauline Pic is a professor of geography at the Université du Québec à Rimouski (Canada), where she holds the Research Chair “Transforming Climate Action” in the geopolitics of seas and oceans. She holds the French agrégation in geography and a PhD in geographical sciences from Université Laval (Québec). Her research focuses on Arctic governance and geopolitics, the governance of maritime spaces, and the geopolitics of maritime data.
